<section id="about"><h2 class="section-head"><span class="section-index">01</span> About Me</h2><p>I’m an undergraduate (2023–2027) pursuing a B.Eng. in <strong>Information Engineering</strong> at <strong>South China University of Technology (SCUT)</strong>.</p><p>I’m a visiting research intern at the <strong>Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (SIAT, CAS)</strong>, advised by <strong>Dr. Wenqi Fang</strong> and <strong>PhD candidate Zhuoyu Wu</strong>. My work explores lightweight medical image segmentation and efficient prediction refinement.</p><p>I’m also a visiting research intern in <strong>Prof. Ming Li’s group at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen</strong>, where I assist with research on multimodal behavioral assessment for autistic children.</p><p>I’m always happy to connect about research, ideas, and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out via <a href="mailto:jingzhizhang569@gmail.com">email</a>!</p></section>
<section id="interests"><h2 class="section-head"><span class="section-index">02</span> Research Interests</h2><p>I am interested in building <strong>efficient, interactive AI systems</strong> that can continuously understand the world and assist people in real time. My research is motivated by a central question: <strong>how can an AI system retain and update the information that matters while operating under limited computational resources?</strong></p><p>My previous work explores lightweight architectures and prediction refinement for medical image segmentation. Building on this foundation, I am interested in extending efficient AI to <strong>streaming multimodal understanding</strong>, including how systems maintain useful context over time, reason about ongoing interactions, and respond appropriately in dynamic environments.</p><p>In the longer term, I hope to develop AI assistants that can learn from continuous sensory input and support people in everyday life—particularly where <strong>timeliness, efficiency, and thoughtful interaction</strong> matter.</p><div class="research-topics"><span>Efficient AI</span><span>Streaming Multimodal Understanding</span><span>Interactive Intelligence</span></div></section>
